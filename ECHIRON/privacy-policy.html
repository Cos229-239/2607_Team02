Echiron Privacy Policy

Effective June 21, 2026

Echiron 1.0 is local-first. It does not transmit, sell, rent, or share your profile, tasks, weekly plan, or focus-session history.

Information stored on your device

Echiron can store the name, preferred name, optional email address, role, goals, planning preference, tasks, task details, categories, priorities, weekly assignments, completion history, theme preference, and focus-session history that you enter. This information is stored locally in the application data area on your device.

Information collected by the developer

Echiron 1.0 does not send personal information or usage analytics to the developer. It contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cloud account, remote database, AI connection, or calendar connection.

Sharing

Echiron 1.0 does not share user information with third parties.

Data deletion

You can delete all Echiron data by opening the Progress screen and selecting “Reset all local data.” Uninstalling Echiron also removes its locally stored application data according to Android's application-storage behavior.

Children

Echiron does not knowingly collect information from children because the application does not transmit user information to the developer. A parent or guardian should supervise device use and any information entered into the application.

Security

Data remains within the protected application storage provided by the operating system. Device-level security still depends on the user's screen lock, operating-system updates, backups, and account security.

Future features

If a future release adds accounts, cloud sync, Google Calendar, notifications, analytics, crash reporting, or AI-provider connections, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

Questions and privacy requests may be submitted through the developer contact shown on Echiron's official Google Play listing.
